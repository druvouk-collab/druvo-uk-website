Privacy Policy

Last updated: 26 November 2026

This policy explains what personal information DRUVO UK collects when you browse or buy from this website, why, who we share it with, and your rights under UK data protection law (UK GDPR and the Data Protection Act 2018).

Who we are

{% include "components/business_identity.html" %}

We are the "controller" of your personal information. Contact us about privacy at {{ contact_email }}.

What we collect

  • Order details: your name, email address, phone number (optional), delivery address, the items you buy and what you paid.
  • Payment: card, Apple Pay and Google Pay payments are handled by Stripe. We receive confirmation of payment and the card type, never your full card number.
  • Messages: what you write to us by email or in the website's chat assistant.
  • Technical data: your IP address, browser type and pages requested, kept briefly in our hosting provider's logs for security.
  • Advertising and analytics data: only if you accept advertising cookies (see Cookies below).

Why we use it, and our legal basis

  • To take payment, deliver your order, handle returns and refunds, and send order emails: performance of our contract with you.
  • To keep sales and tax records for 6 years: legal obligation.
  • To prevent fraud, keep the website secure and answer your questions: our legitimate interests.
  • Advertising and analytics cookies: your consent, which you can withdraw at any time.

We don't sell your information, and we don't send marketing emails unless you've opted in.

Who we share it with

Only the companies that help us run the shop, each under a contract that protects your data:

  • Stripe — payments and fraud checks.
  • Render — website hosting (servers in the EU) and our order database.
  • Our email provider (Resend) — sending order, dispatch and refund emails.
  • Delivery companies (for example Royal Mail) — your name and address to deliver your parcel.
  • OpenAI — powers the chat assistant; it receives only what you type in the chat. Please don't share card details there.
  • Google — web fonts, and Google Analytics only if you accept cookies.
  • Meta, TikTok, Snapchat — only if you accept advertising cookies.

Some of these companies process data in the United States. Where that happens, the transfer is protected by the UK International Data Transfer Addendum, standard contractual clauses or the UK–US data bridge.

Cookies and local storage

This website uses essential browser storage for your basket, checkout and session. These are needed for the shop to work and do not need consent.

How long we keep it

  • Orders, payments and refunds: 6 years after the end of the tax year, as required for tax records.
  • Emails and chat messages: up to 2 years after our last contact, unless they relate to an order.
  • Hosting security logs: no longer than 30 days.

Your rights

You can ask us for a copy of your information, to correct it, to delete it (where we don't need to keep it by law), to restrict or object to how we use it, and to move it to another provider. Email {{ contact_email }}; we'll reply within one month. If you're unhappy with how we've handled your information you can complain to the Information Commissioner's Office: ico.org.uk/make-a-complaint, 0303 123 1113.

Security

The website uses HTTPS, payments are handled by Stripe, and access to order data is limited to the shop owner and protected by two-step sign-in.

Children

This shop is for adults. We don't knowingly collect information from children under 13.

Changes

If we change this policy we'll update the date at the top.
