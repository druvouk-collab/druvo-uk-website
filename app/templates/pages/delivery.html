If something goes wrong

  • If your parcel hasn't arrived within 7 working days of dispatch, email us and we'll trace it with the carrier. If it's lost, we'll send a replacement (if we have one) or a full refund.

  • Please check your delivery address carefully. If a parcel is returned to us because the address was wrong, we can resend it once you've paid the postage again.
