Shipping & Returns

Everything you need to know about UK delivery and returning items to DRUVO UK.

UK delivery

We ship across the United Kingdom using tracked services.

  • Standard UK delivery: 2–4 working days — £3.99 (free on orders over £75)
  • Express UK delivery: 1–2 working days — £6.99

You will receive a tracking number by email once your order has been dispatched. For full delivery details see our delivery information page.

Returns

You may return eligible items within 14 days of delivery under UK consumer regulations, provided items are unworn, with tags attached, and in the same condition as received.

To start a return, contact {{ contact_email }} with your order number. Return postage is the customer’s responsibility unless the item is faulty or not as described.

Refunds are processed within 5–10 working days after we receive and inspect the return. See our full returns policy.
